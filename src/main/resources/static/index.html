The Baker

A kitchen table business, baked one order at a time.

Mani's Cakes started in Ramya.M's own kitchen with a single red velvet recipe and a handful of neighbours who kept asking for more. Today it's grown into a full home-bakery menu — but the process hasn't changed: every cake is still mixed, baked, and frosted by Ramya herself, in small batches, using fresh dairy and no preservatives.

Ramya trained in classical baking before coming home to build something of her own, and she still tastes every batter before it goes into the oven. If a cake isn't right, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

"I don't bake to sell cakes. I bake so that someone's celebration has one less thing to worry about." — Ramya.M
